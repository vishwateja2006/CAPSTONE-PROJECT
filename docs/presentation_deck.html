6. VIVA DEFENSE PREPARATION

Frequently Asked Defense Questions

Q: Why Max-Min fairness over greedy allocation?

A: Greedy allocation starves distant families with medical emergencies. Max-Min maximizes the minimum fulfillment ratio across all victims, guaranteeing baseline survival kits to all.

Q: How do you prevent trucks from driving into floodwaters?

A: Satellite tiles segment flood polygons. Our routing algorithm runs ray-casting intersection checks; if a leg intersects flood polygons, tangent detours are inserted around the perimeter.

Q: What if volunteers lose cell connectivity?

A: Offline Synchronization Queue stores drop-offs in local storage and batch reconciles with cryptographic PoD receipts once connectivity resumes.
